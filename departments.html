Orthopaedics

Move freely again.

Bones, joints, spine and injuries — with a recovery plan you can follow.

Come to us for

  • Knee, back, neck and shoulder pain
  • Fractures, sprains and sports injuries

05 / 06 · NE

Neurology

Understand the signals.

Headaches, dizziness, numbness, seizures, memory and sleep.

Come to us for

  • Migraine, headache and dizziness
  • Numbness, tingling, weakness or tremor
  • Seizures, memory and sleep concerns

Call emergency instead if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech
  • The worst headache of your life, suddenly
  • A seizure lasting more than five minutes

06 / 06 · PE

Pediatrics

Care that grows with them.

Newborns to teens — illness, growth, vaccines and reassurance.

Come to us for

  • Fever, cough, colds and tummy upsets
  • Vaccinations, growth and nutrition
  • Newborn and developmental check-ups

Call emergency instead if

  • Difficulty breathing or bluish lips
  • Unusually drowsy, floppy or hard to wake
  • A fit, or no wet nappy for many hours
