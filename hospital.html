Your visit

Know what happens next.

From the first message to the follow-up, four calm steps.

  1. 01

    Request

    Use the concierge, WhatsApp or a call. Tell us the concern in a line.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    The desk confirms your doctor and exact time by phone.

  3. 03

    Arrive

    Come 15 minutes early to register at the front desk.

  4. 04

    Follow up

    Leave with a written plan and your next visit booked if you need one.

Bring with you

  • Photo ID and your insurance card
  • Previous reports, scans and prescriptions
  • A list of medicines you take now
  • A family member, if you’d like one with you
